Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clip Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer. It uploads video clips that the owner chooses to the owner's own TikTok account using TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile information (your TikTok user ID, display name and avatar) to confirm which account is signed in.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to the signed-in account.
  • The posting settings TikTok returns for the account, such as allowed privacy levels and maximum video length.

How information is stored

The TikTok access token and refresh token are stored only on the owner's computer, so the app can upload without signing in each time. Video files, titles and captions stay on the owner's computer until they are uploaded to TikTok. The app has no server and keeps no database.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any information with anyone. Information is sent only to TikTok, as needed to upload videos.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (wording may vary). Deleting the stored token file on the computer also removes the app's access.
